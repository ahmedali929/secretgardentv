# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Muslim parents (and grandparents, aunts/uncles) of children roughly ages 3–9, looking for screen time they can trust: media that entertains like mainstream cartoons but plants Islamic character, Arabic, and faith. They arrive curious and slightly skeptical ("is this actually good, or just well-meaning?") and decide in seconds whether the studio feels real and high-craft. The child is often watching over their shoulder and reacts to the characters on screen.

## Product Purpose
Secret Garden TV is an Islamic educational media studio. The landing page is the front door: it has to make a parent feel the production quality is real, introduce the show universe and its characters, and convert interest into a newsletter signup ("be the first to enter the Secret Garden") and donations. Success = the page feels like a beloved animation studio's site (Bluey-tier craft), not a nonprofit brochure, and parents trust it enough to subscribe.

## Brand Personality
Warm, wondrous, rooted. The voice is a kind storyteller, not a curriculum. Playful and bright for the child, reassuring and substantive for the parent. The "garden" is the central metaphor: every child has a garden within, and stories are how it's tended. Faith is woven in naturally, never preachy.

## Anti-references
- Generic nonprofit/charity template (stock-photo grids, donate-bar, "we are a 501c3" energy).
- The original secretgardentv.com Bootstrap theme (KidsCamp-style stock template, owl carousels, clip-art icons).
- Cartoon chaos: rainbow Comic-Sans overload, balloon clutter, seizure-bright primaries.
- Cold/clinical edtech (Inter on white, SaaS gradients).
- Cream/beige "warm editorial" body background (the 2026 AI default).

## Design Principles
1. **Show the garden, don't describe it.** The characters and world carry the page; copy stays short and specific.
2. **Two readers at once.** Every fold delights the watching child and reassures the deciding parent.
3. **Craft is the pitch.** Motion, depth, and polish ARE the argument that the shows are well made.
4. **Faith is the soil, not a banner.** Islamic character shows through warmth, family, and the show lineup, not slogans.
5. **One world, layered.** Foliage, depth, and parallax make a single coherent place you scroll through, not a stack of sections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (no parallax/auto-motion; crossfades only). Keyboard-navigable nav, cards, and newsletter form. Respect that many users are parents on phones; mobile-first and fast.
