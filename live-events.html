Got Questions?

Organizer Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about technical requirements, travel, and logistics.

What age groups are the live shows best for? expand_more
Our live stage programming is calibrated primarily for children aged 4 to 12. Because our storylines are rooted in warmth, comedy, and high-fidelity music, toddlers, teenagers, and parents also find themselves captivated. For schools, we can conduct separated sessions for lower and upper elementary.
What technical or AV equipment do you require? expand_more
We provide our own character props, science demonstration materials, and show soundtracks on digital media. The venue typically needs to provide:
  • A standard sound system with Bluetooth or 3.5mm input
  • Two wireless handheld or lapel microphones
  • A projection screen or LED wall for show backdrops (optional but recommended)
  • A small staging area or cleared gymnasium floor
Do you travel out-of-state or internationally? expand_more
Yes! Secret Garden TV tours extensively across the United States and Canada throughout the academic year and school holiday seasons. We also accept select international tour invitations (UK, Gulf, Australia) for coordinated community tour blocks.
Is there an honorarium fee for non-profit masjids? expand_more
We operate on a mission-first model. We offer flexible honorarium options, ticketed-split models, and donor-sponsored visits so that lack of funds never prevents a community from experiencing the Garden. Let us know your venue's capacity and we will formulate a workable plan together!
