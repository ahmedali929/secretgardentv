  • Bringing the Garden to your community through live children's programs, workshops, and educator trainings.

Bring Secret Garden TV to Your Community

Secret Garden TV offers live children's programs, storytime gatherings, educator trainings, and family/community workshops rooted in Islamic values, joyful learning, imagination, reflection, and play.

Our live events are designed to help children, families, and educators encounter Islam through stories, wonder, creativity, and the signs of Allah in the world around us.

Our Live Event Offerings

Classroom of Allah's Signs

A guided Allah's Secret Signs screening paired with a hands-on activity that helps children encounter an ayah through reflection, creativity, and play.

Garden Educator Training

Professional development for teachers, Sunday school educators, parents, and mentors rooted in SGTV's whole-child approach to Islamic education, imagination, presence, and purposeful learning.

Ayatology: Knowing the Signs

A lecture or workshop for parents, educators, and community leaders introducing Ayatology and SGTV's core values as a framework for raising conscious, compassionate, and purposeful children.

Secret Garden Storytime

A live children's book read-aloud event featuring Secret Garden TV physical books, paired with a guided activity that brings story, character, and wonder into a warm community setting.

Custom SGTV Event

Not sure which format is right? We can recommend the best event design for your community's needs, audience, and goals.
